This project explores how encounters and relationships shape our identities over time. Using p5.js, I created a generative environment where colorful abstract figures, representing individual souls, move freely and interact with one another.

When two souls approach each other, a red thread connects them, symbolizing human relationships and the idea of destiny. As they get closer, their colors blend and leave small marks on each other's bodies, representing the lasting influence of encounters.

The environment follows an automatic day-and-night cycle, affecting both the background and the movement speed of the souls. When a soul crosses the canvas boundary, it changes color and begins a new cycle.

Through this project, I want to explore how individuals continuously change through relationships, time, and their surrounding environment.
